---
title: "Unit Details and related content"
description: "How Devour presents an independent Unit, its Topics, and related Unit or Article results."
---

An Article renders Units as part of its composition, while Unit Details presents one Unit as an independent context surface. Core owns canonical Unit identity, Graf ordering, and retrieval semantics; see [Units and Grafs](/developer/core/units) and [Unit queries](/developer/core/unit-queries).

## Unit Details

The surface loads one exact Unit ID and distinguishes loading, failure, unavailable, and successful results. A successful result renders canonical Grafs in order and preserves their text with whitespace. The originating Article headline is an action that opens that exact Article ID through workspace navigation.

Unit Topics are adapted to the public Topics block. Selecting one opens a Topic context successor, and the shared Article Topic filters control which chips are visible. The Unit module owns the content; the [workspace adapter](https://github.com/coreyconner/snewse/blob/master/apps/devour/src/app/workspace/surfaces/UnitDetailsWorkspaceAdapter.tsx) owns closing the pane and translating navigation requests.

## Related content module

The related-content module supplies two distinct presentations:

- **Related Units** appear within Unit Details. Each result includes the matched Unit's canonical text and minimal originating Article context. Selecting a result opens the originating Article by exact ID.
- **Related Articles** occupy their own context surface opened from an Article. Results use the shared Article Stream and replace the current Article target when selected.

Related Unit results paginate through the module's query state and keep their selection local to the current result set. Loading, retry, empty, and load-more states are explicit. See [`RelatedUnitsSurface`](https://github.com/coreyconner/snewse/blob/master/apps/devour/src/modules/related-content/RelatedUnitsSurface.tsx) and [`RelatedArticleList`](https://github.com/coreyconner/snewse/blob/master/apps/devour/src/modules/related-content/RelatedArticleList.tsx).

<Note>
  Minimal Article context on a related Unit result does not establish Article access. Opening the Article still passes through the normal exact-ID query and private unavailable behavior.
</Note>

Current workspace navigation opens the Article but does not carry a matched-Unit target into it. That limit is visible at the related-content selection boundary and should not be inferred as passage navigation.
